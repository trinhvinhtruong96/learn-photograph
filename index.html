How to use this roadmap

  1. Read. Each lesson starts with the idea in one sentence.
  2. Play. Change one setting in the simulator and watch what happens.
  3. Practice. Go out and do the tasks with your camera.
  4. Review. Compare your photos side by side in darktable.
  5. Tick. Mark a task done when you meet its check.

Plan on 3 to 5 hours a week. Stages 1 to 6 build on each other, so take them in order. After that, pick the tracks in any order.
